關於這個練習ABOUT THIS TOOL

這是一個受正念（Mindfulness）、自我覺察（Self-awareness）、自我慈悲（Self-compassion）與認知彈性（Cognitive Flexibility）等心理學概念啟發的日常自我反思工具。

本工具僅供日常自我覺察與個人反思使用，並非經臨床驗證的心理測驗，也不提供心理診斷或治療，無法取代專業心理諮商或醫療服務。

每個人都有不同的生活經驗與感受，這裡沒有標準答案。如果某個問題讓你感到不舒服，可以隨時停止練習。

This tool is inspired by psychological concepts such as mindfulness, self-awareness, self-compassion, and cognitive flexibility.

It is designed for everyday self-reflection. It is not a clinically validated psychological assessment, does not provide diagnosis or treatment, and is not a substitute for professional mental health or medical care.

Everyone has different experiences and feelings. There are no right or wrong answers. If any prompt makes you uncomfortable, feel free to stop at any time.
